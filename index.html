Welcome!

Our group uses computational modeling and mathematical theory to understand the geometric and topological properties of soft, active, and biological materials. We are interested in how topological structures in ordered phases form, interact, morph, may be manipulated, and alter macroscopic properties of the material. We are also interested in how topology and geometry may be used to model and characterize active and biological systems out of equilibrium. We are heavily motivated by experiments and try to make cross-disciplinary analogies that benefit our understanding of the underlying physics. If you want to learn a bit more about our current interests, see our Research page, or feel free to reach out!
